## Design Specificity Verdict

Combo Forge has a strong, authored visual identity. The forge theme — charcoal void, gold accents, parchment cards — is carried consistently across every screen. This is NOT category-interchangeable: you couldn't swap this onto a puzzle game or a fitness tracker without it feeling wrong. The design is grounded in the product's metaphor (heat, craft, darkness) and the color system enforces restraint (gold is rare and authoritative). The card faces as physical parchment objects against the dark is a distinctive touch that creates genuine product character.

Deterministic scan found 12 instances of #241a05 (dark brown text on gold buttons) — consistent and intentional but undocumented in DESIGN.md.

## What's Working

1. The Gold Rule is enforced beautifully — Forge Gold appears on ≤15% of every screen.
2. Escape hatches are excellent — every screen has back, forge has cancel, selections have clear, modals have LATER.
3. The "so close" Zeigarnik effect on Run Lost — psychologically precise near-miss motivation.

## Priority Issues

[P1] No onboarding or contextual help — 7+ interconnected systems with zero in-game guidance.
[P2] Combo multiplier visually weak during play — the core mechanic is a small pill.
[P2] Forge deck picker overwhelming — 40 cards in a dense grid with no filtering.
[P2] Relic effects invisible during gameplay — scoring modifiers de-emphasized when they matter most.
[P3] #241a05 undocumented in design system — 12 instances across all screens.
